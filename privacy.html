Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

ETF Insight Automation is a personal-use application for organizing ETF research materials.

Information Accessed

The application accesses authorized Google Drive files and folders to upload and organize research documents.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to organize research files for the application owner.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell user information or share it with advertisers.

Data Storage and Security

Research files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Authorization credentials are used to maintain access.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections settings.
